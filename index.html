100% Offline Gameplay

Play anywhere, on flights, subways, or without cellular data. High scores and unlocked skins are saved right on your device.

Flame Engine & 60 FPS

Powered by Flutter 3.x and the Flame Game Engine for ultra-crisp neon particle animations and zero input delay.

Fair Ads & Free Rewards

No paywalls or pay-to-win. Optional rewarded videos let you revive or unlock daily coin chests (up to 5 chests/day).

COMPLIANCE & ARCHITECTURE

Pixel Pocket adheres strictly to Google Play Policies and AdMob Better Ads Guidelines.

Non-intrusive bottom banner adhering to safe zones and preventing accidental clicks.

AdFrequencyController

Smart Interstitial capping: only triggers after at least 3 completed rounds and 120s cooldown.

Game music automatically pauses during rewarded video ads to provide a seamless user experience.

Local Storage Only

No personal records or user identifiers stored on private remote servers.
