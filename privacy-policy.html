Privacy Policy for Pixel Pocket: Arcade Mini

Last updated: November 2025 • Package: com.pixelpocket.arcademini
Privacy Overview: Pixel Pocket: Arcade Mini is designed as an offline ad-supported arcade game. We do not require any registration, do not collect personal identities, and all game scores and unlocked skins are kept strictly on your local device.

Pixel Pocket: Arcade Mini ("the App", "we", "our", or "us") is developed and offered as an Ad-Supported casual gaming service. This SERVICE is provided at no cost and is intended for use as is.

This page informs players regarding our policies with the collection, use, and disclosure of personal information for anyone deciding to download, install, or play our game.

1. Information Collection and Storage

No Account or Personal Identity Required: The App does NOT collect, ask for, or store any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your real name, email address, physical location, phone number, contacts, or payment credentials.

Local Storage Only: All game data—including your 24 mini-game high scores, earned coins, unlocked neon skins, sound preferences, and achievements—are stored locally on your device via standard application preferences (SharedPreferences). We do not operate custom backend database servers to store user profiles.

2. Third-Party Advertising & Services (Google AdMob)

To keep this multi-game collection 100% free for all players worldwide, the App incorporates third-party advertising SDKs provided by Google LLC.

Third-party software development kits (SDKs) utilized by the App:

  • Google Play Services (Core device runtime and security verification)
  • Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK for Banner, Interstitial, and Rewarded Video Ads)

What Google AdMob May Automatically Collect:

In accordance with Google's privacy policies, Google AdMob may automatically collect non-personally identifiable technical diagnostic and advertising telemetry from your device, including:

  • Device Identifiers: Google Advertising ID (GAID on Android) or Identifier for Advertisers (IDFA on iOS), and general device hardware specs (e.g., screen resolution, OS version).
  • Coarse Location & Network Info: IP address (utilized in anonymized fashion to infer general country or city for localized ad delivery).
  • Ad Interaction & Performance: Impressions, views, clicks, and crash diagnostic logs to optimize delivery and prevent invalid traffic/click fraud.

For further details regarding how Google processes user data, please consult Google's official privacy and terms documentation:

  • Google Privacy Policy & Terms of Service
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services

3. Advertising Controls & User Opt-Out

Players have full control over personalized advertising. You may reset or opt-out of personalized ads directly through your device's operating system settings:

  • On Android: Open Settings > Google > Ads (or Privacy > Ads). Select "Reset advertising ID" or "Delete advertising ID".
  • On iOS: Open Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking, and disable "Allow Apps to Request to Track".

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA & Family Policy Compliance)

Pixel Pocket: Arcade Mini is intended for a general audience. We do not knowingly solicit or collect personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a minor has provided any personal information, please notify us immediately so that appropriate measures can be taken.

5. In-Game Virtual Currency (Coins) & Rewarded Ads

Coins earned from completing mini-game challenges or viewing optional rewarded video ads are strictly virtual items. They possess zero real-world monetary value and cannot be exchanged, transferred, or refunded for real currency.

6. Data Security

Because we do not transmit user gameplay profiles to remote servers, your gameplay data remains confined to your physical device. All ad requests handled by Google AdMob are encrypted via industry-standard Transport Layer Security (TLS / HTTPS).

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We advise you to review this page periodically for any updates. Changes are effective immediately upon posting to this website.
